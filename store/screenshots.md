# Screenshot checklist

iPhone sizes App Store Connect requires: 6.7" (1290×2796 or 6.9" equiv) and
6.5" (1284×2778). Play wants at least 2 phone shots (1080×1920 min) plus a
1024×500 feature graphic. Shoot on-device or simulator with light theme, full
battery, 9:41, Do Not Disturb.

## Shot list (order = listing order)

1. **Quiz card** — a question mid-answer, progress bar partway.
   Caption: "22 issues. Agree to disagree."
2. **Result screen** — spectrum bar with the user's pin + match dots.
   Caption: "Your placement, scored by pillar."
3. **Match list** — ranked politicians with match %.
   Caption: "Ranked by distance, not donations."
4. **Your ballot** — match list filtered to a state + district.
   Caption: "Just the people you can vote for."
5. **Politician detail** — a well-known figure's issue positions or vote record.
   Caption: "The receipts behind the score."
6. **Friend compare / Venn** — the overlap diagram.
   Caption: "Compare with a friend, in the same room or by link."
7. **Privacy card** — transfer screen's encrypted-link text or methodology.
   Caption: "Encrypted on your phone before it ever leaves."

## Feature graphic (Play)

Slate-paper background, the ballot-box mark left, wordmark right, spectrum
rail beneath — match `web/app/icon.png` and `opengraph-image.tsx` styling.
