## Full description

Find out where you actually land — and who actually matches.

Voteaxis is a private political-spectrum app. Answer a short quiz on 22 issues
and see your position on the liberal–conservative spectrum, scored by pillar —
economy, rights, health, justice, and the world. Then compare yourself against
738 real politicians scored on their votes and public positions.

Your answers never leave your phone. No account, no email, no ads, no
trackers. Quiz results live in encrypted device storage; sharing is opt-in and
can be sealed with a password before it goes anywhere.

• Your ballot: pick your state and congressional district, and matches narrow
  to candidates you can actually vote for.
• Search 738 politicians by name; browse issue positions and vote records.
• Friend compare: sit together and swap a code — or share an encrypted link.
• Issue of the Day widget and an optional local daily reminder.
• Register to vote or check your registration via vote.gov.
• Dark mode, TalkBack support, reduced-motion option.

Free. Made by The Greigh Studios LLC. Methodology: https://voteaxis.org/methodology
