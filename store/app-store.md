## Description

Find out where you actually land — and who actually matches.

Voteaxis is a private political-spectrum app. Answer a short quiz on 22 issues
and see your position on the liberal–conservative spectrum, scored by pillar —
economy, rights, health, justice, and the world. Then compare yourself against
738 real politicians: presidents, governors, senators, and representatives,
scored on their votes and public positions, not vibes.

EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

Your answers are scored on your device and stored in your phone's secure
storage. There is no account, no email, no tracker, no ads. If you share a
result, you choose what goes up — and you can encrypt it with a password only
you hold before it leaves your hands.

YOUR BALLOT

Pick your state and congressional district and the match list narrows to the
people you can actually vote for. Search any name in the roster. Browse
politicians' issue positions, vote records, and how their score moved across
congresses.

COMPARE WITH FRIENDS

Sit down with a friend and compare spectra with a code — coordinates go point
to point, not to our servers. Or share a link: a password seals your result on
your phone first, so the server stores bytes it cannot read.

CIVIC BY DESIGN

- Issue of the Day widget and optional daily reminder — all local
- Every score cites its basis; methodology published at voteaxis.org
- Register to vote or check your registration via vote.gov
- Dark mode, VoiceOver labels, reduced-motion option

Made by The Greigh Studios LLC. Free. No subscriptions, no data for sale —
there is no data to sell.
